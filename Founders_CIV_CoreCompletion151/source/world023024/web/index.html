Integrity requirements

Physical ledgers

All matter and energy moves must debit an existing account; material state changes pay one registered toll. An unfunded transaction fails atomically.

Belief causality

Religion cannot directly improve rainfall or crop yields. An effect must be mediated by real actions, teaching, cooperation or resource management.

Social provenance

Source-tagged knowledge transmission cannot grant mastered practical skills without experience and practice.

Institutional legitimacy

Member count, operating resources, real rules, costs and dissent must be visible—not a free institution created by score alone.

What this release does not claim

The current code remains an abstract engineering prototype. It cannot establish empirical truths about historical religion or human societies, ecological sustainability across generations, or real chemical thermodynamics. A visually convincing animation is not validation of an unimplemented physical rule.
