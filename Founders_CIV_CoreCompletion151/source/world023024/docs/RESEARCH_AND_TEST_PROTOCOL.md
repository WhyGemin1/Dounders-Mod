# Operational Test and Research Integrity Protocol — Founders-CIV

**Invariant:** a narrative interpretation is not proof of a causal mechanism. Every physical claim must be auditable and every social-effect claim must connect beliefs, decisions, behavior, and material outcomes.

## Mechanical gates

1. All mass and energy accounts remain nonnegative within floating-point tolerance, and totals balance except explicit Wellspring inflow.
2. A material-state transition pays exactly one funded toll; mere transfer or ownership change pays none.
3. Every event ID is unique and connects to an observable day and actor/system source. Physical actions may include a transaction ID.
4. Actor knowledge and simulation truth remain distinct. Information exposure does not directly increase trained skills.
5. A named institution must have identifiable people, rules and pool; numerical consensus alone cannot materialize a granary.
6. Identical seed/configuration produces identical simulated outputs under different Python process hash seeds.
7. Dashboard coordinate jitter is not used to calculate movement, targeting, disease, shelter or material stocks.

## Report format

Record version hash, code release, all parameter values, seed, factor arm, time horizon, control/shock conditions, event counts, model violations, primary/secondary metrics, confidence intervals across *seed blocks*, outliers, who bears costs, negative controls, and at least three plausible falsifiers for each conceptual claim.

## Scientific restraint

Simulation experiments demonstrate behavior of the programmed model. They do not establish that spirituality necessarily generates cooperation, that religion always regulates ecology, or that the fictional Soul Network corresponds to real physics. Research documents guide hypotheses but should not be treated as empirical validation unless their supporting primary sources are independently assessed.
