# Founders-CIV v0.18 — Canonical Systems Specification

**Status date:** 5 November 2026  
**Release:** v0.18 Canon & Observatory (v0.17 validated core + read-only dashboard)  
**Scope:** Consolidation of all established simulation rules known from the current project discussion, retained research specifications, and earlier released code. *Canon* is not synonymous with *implemented*. Fictional world and game rules are separate from empirical scientific theories.

## 1. What is canonical, and what is experimental?

A **canonical design rule** is a rule the world/simulation is intended to obey. A **implemented mechanic** is a testable part of the current Python core. A **provisional parameter** is a number chosen to make a test runnable; it is not canon and may change after sensitivity studies. An **unimplemented design** is described but is not yet simulated. The register records these distinctions explicitly.

### Canonical operating principles

1. **Individual agency:** all Founders, Spirits, mortal creations, and animals have persistent identity and learn from experience. Their roles do not lock their future skills.
2. **Physical conservation:** physical resources must move between accounts, not duplicate when owned or harvested. Energy entering from the Wellspring is declared; the House does not create sunshine without drawing on funded reserves.
3. **Material transformation:** the universal toll is incurred only when material changes state; movement, observation and ownership changes alone do not incur it.
4. **Environmental limitation:** plants require both energy and matter; animals have individual habitat, physiological, biological and learned constraints. Migration can avert local scarcity but does not guarantee survival.
5. **Cognitive complexity:** motivations include five needs, fear interpretation, values, willingness and capability, bounded knowledge, personal goals, memory and learned skills. These can conflict.
6. **Social causality:** family, friends, relatives, caretakers, neighbors, peers and mentors affect what information actors hear, trust, practice and pass on. Evidence and competence remain separate from social confidence.
7. **Spirituality and religion:** individual spiritual orientation concerns how needs/fear become interpreted convictions and commitments; communicated, shared symbolic understanding can develop into religion and collective obligations. Belief does not directly change physical crop yield absent a real mechanism.
8. **Institutional organization:** members, material resources, rules, RSW (normative meaning) and PSW (instrumental capacity) operate together; institutions can coordinate, impose costs, adapt, capture, fail or dissolve.
9. **Soul Network development:** action and remembered experience develop skills and executive schemas; network ability requires its own documented prerequisites and appropriate spiritual validation. Unusual overload is exceptional, not routine training failure.
10. **Scientific honesty:** all proposed emergence claims require event-level causal mechanisms, counterfactual controls, failure criteria, and distributional analysis; fictional simulation outcomes are not confirmation of real human history.

## 2. Universe and ontology

The starting world is the House on a floating, largely empty island, with a waterfall and river supplied by the Wellspring. The House contains a fountain that sustains Founders. Exactly **100 Founders** are intended, each with an individual identity, non-linear learnable role, and random close support partner. The Founder population presently is **two placeholders**; 100-agent simulation remains planned. Their checkpoint mechanism saves learned development in the House; on death a Founder returns to the last checkpoint, losing memory and skills gained after it. This checkpoint system is canon but not yet executable.

Founder abilities require energy: they may generate mortal creations, worlds, connected realms and energy-dependent dimensions. Mortal creations are born, age, reproduce, die and must inhabit worlds, ordinarily without access to the House. Dimensions may collapse when unfunded. Spirits consist of six role classes: Knights, Emperors, Assassins, Castors, Archers and Berserkers. Those Spirit actors are different entities from the spiritual validation of Soul Arts.

The **Wellspring**, House energy reserve, sunlight, world stocks, ten-layer Underworld and universe-scale toll accounting form one circulation system. The House buffers returned energy and independent Wellspring inflow. A minimum daylight or light emission should be backed by reserves; changing a world's daylight fraction affects its access to energy rather than creating extra energy. Percentages and coefficients are experimental until calibrated.

## 3. Physics and material data

A persistent material resource has mass/composition, location, status, ownership, condition, accessible energy, embodied energy and a history of transformations. Examples include water, minerals, soil, nutrients, living plants, felled trees, lumber, food, structures, inventories, corpses, oils and inhalants. Fine elemental and chemical grids remain specified only: the live core conserves **one abstract material quantity**, not real carbon/nitrogen/oxygen atoms.

An energy ledger tracks House reserve, sunlight, usable reserves, chemical reservoirs, embodied energy, environment/heat and ten Underworld layers. For normal processes, energy remains conserved within the defined universe plus the Wellspring boundary. Universal toll is charged **exactly once** upon changes of material state such as growth, digestion, cooking, crafting, death and decay. The current *test* toll equals 10% of associated work energy, routed 20% to world, 10% to Underworld and 70% to House; these ratios are not permanent canon. Work energy cannot come from an unfunded account. Changes in location/ownership and simple sunlight absorption do not independently trigger toll.

Biological consumption must transfer existing food material into body/fuel/waste and account for available chemical energy. Cooking can improve bioavailability but not generate energy or material from nothing. Water and nutrient cycling require their own independently conserved material registers once added. Fusion or mass defect must explicitly account for mass-energy equivalence and binding energy; the current engine does not simulate nuclear mechanics.

## 4. Time, spatial hierarchy and ecology

Worlds are spatially organized through **overlapping scales**: element, material, object/space, habitat patch, region, world and connected realm. These are indexes/views of one stock, not additional independent quantities. The current demonstration contains four named patches in each of two worlds. UI dots have illustrative positions only within the patch; they are *not* fine-grained simulation coordinates.

All animals are actors with age, physiology, energy reserves, diet, learned abilities, experience memory, Soul Networks and possible social relationships. Reproduction depends on species physiology, maturity, environment, mate availability, condition, pregnancy or equivalent mechanisms, and infant survival—not surplus prey energy alone. Animal learning, cultural transmission and inherited biological evolution are distinct yet interacting processes. Migration can follow seasonal resources, cultural routes and risk; local extinction differs from global extinction.

Hunting is organized into search, approach, capture, consume and learning. Pack predators gain potential cooperative techniques through interaction and practice. Ambush hunters learn concealment, positioning, grappling; herbivores learn foraging, shelter, migration and danger response. Species traits bound capacities but do not guarantee expertise. A 30% maximum hunt success is **not** canonical: rates depend on species, prey and context. Scavengers track carcasses; decomposers return material to nutrient pathways. A food web must pass population persistence tests across multiple generations before being considered stable.

## 5. Cognition, Fear Logic and spiritual motivation

Five motivational domains: physiological needs, safety, love/belonging, esteem/status or competence, and self-development. Need expression is species-specific. Motivations are not a rigid hierarchy. Fear Logic does **not** assign all actions to fear or define fear solely as anticipated loss; it examines how fear relates to convictions, enjoyment, values and desired action. An actor may avoid, confront, enjoy, reinterpret or tolerate fear. Its spiritual orientation connects needs, fear, meaning, values, willingness and realistic ability to commit. Long-term promises and plans should persist across individual actions.

Agent decisions must proceed from partial perception and remembered beliefs, through goals and candidate actions, to feasibility, commitment and experienced outcome. Actor opinions are not the same as world truth. Actor experience events contain day, context, action, outcome, skill/schema connections, interpreted meaning and sources. Randomness should be seeded and independent wherever possible to preserve matched experimental baselines.

## 6. Universal Soul Network

Soul Networks organize intrinsic/extrinsic Nodes, executive schemas, trained States, and intrinsic Zodiac Temple modes with Ascendant/Descendant development. Ordinary cognitive schemas are not yet reality-altering abilities. A **Kingdom** coordinates at least three connected States; a **Nation** at least four States in an organizational structure; a **Realm** at least four Kingdoms and an Emperor Node. Spiritual validation through vows/commitment applies where required for power activation. Exceptional overload arises only when externally manipulated energy exceeds supplied energy and available control/stabilization; ordinary practice need not overload. These are designed magical rules, **not claims about real quantum physics**. The live engine implements only elementary practice and Node threshold events.

## 7. Social networks and intergenerational knowledge

Each agent has directed, overlapping relationships: parent, guardian, sibling, relative, peer/friend, neighbor, mentor/apprentice, leader/follower, rival, partner and stranger. Edge attributes may include trust, familiarity, attachment, respect, dependence, contact frequency, reciprocity, shared experience, and agreement. A parent may teach survival while a skilled hunter teaches tracking. Biological kinship does not guarantee affection or reliability. Social network topology shapes which information can reach an actor, how often it is reinforced, and how practice is supported. Exposure, belief adoption, conformity, observation, practical mastery and spiritual commitment are distinct outcomes.

The current engine has directed family/friend/neighbor/mentor edges and two concrete information claims. It has **no** child life-cycle, real parenthood, complex dispute reasoning or intergenerational reproduction yet.

## 8. RSW, PSW, religion and CIV-OS governance

RSW is the normative-symbolic aspect of personal and collective organization: meaning, sacred objects, limits, values, legitimacy and obligations. PSW is the instrumental aspect: tools, production, coordination, logistics, training and institutional capacity. Both aspects apply to *every* institution. Families, granaries, schools, hunting packs, religious communities, guilds and councils should acquire real membership, resource accounts, activities, governance and continuity. Religion develops when spiritually meaningful interpretations are communicated and become durable collective beliefs and practices; agreement is never automatic. Dissent, error correction, coercion and schisms must be possible.

CIV-OS analyses distinguish L1 substrate (energy/ecology), L1b accumulated capital (tools/skills/infrastructure), L2 RSW and PSW aspects, and L3 polycentric governance. Evaluation must report an institution's sacred object, public story versus operational rules, who receives benefit and bears cost, resilience or chokepoints, legitimacy, and plausible rival causal mechanisms. Pathologies—drift, hollowing, rigidity, calibration, capture, brittleness—are *diagnostic hypotheses*, not automatic event counters.

## 9. Experiment and validation protocol

The v0.16 factorial study defines four independent mechanisms: **S** (diverse social networks), **F** (personal Fear Logic and spiritual commitments), **B** (shared beliefs), **I** (institutional feedback). The registered trial is 16 arms × 30 matched seeds × 720 days = **480 long runs**; this release does not claim those runs occurred. Two identical material worlds, each with two 24-creation settlements, provide a pilot setup with founder-controlled sunlight policies and shared Wellspring/House boundary. Every test should include a mechanical unit gate, isolated subsystem gate, coupled-system test, matched comparative arms, adversarial controls and failures set in advance.

Run requirements: balance audits; event provenance; no automatic institutional productivity bonuses; knowledge source vs skill practice separation; spatial stock identity; per-group costs/benefits; reproducibility across hash seeds; descriptive statistics with uncertainties for paired replicates; counterarguments and held-out scenarios. For each claim, identify the chain of actions that made the effect possible. Multi-generational ecology and independent institutional behavior are **still required before confirmatory testing**.

## 10. Rule-by-rule implementation register

The following entries come from the current Python canon register and are bundled in the UI's **Canon register** tab.

### Universe

**House, Wellspring & the Ten-Layer Underworld — PARTIAL**  
A Wellspring supplies energy to the House, independently of energy returning from the ten-layer Underworld. The House buffers incoming energy and distributes sunlight to connected worlds; Founder policy can alter daylight exposure without creating energy.  
*Python:* `core.engine.Simulation._daily_sun`  
*Gap:* Reserve feedback is simplified; minimum sunlight and allocation policy are experimental. No physically detailed dimensional transport.

**100 individual Founders and checkpoints — SPECIFIED**  
One hundred immortal individual Founders begin around the House, each with a randomly assigned support partner. They absorb river energy, develop abilities through experience, and may create worlds, creations, realms and dimensions. Upon death a Founder returns to its most recent saved House checkpoint, losing unsaved development.  
*Python:* `Founders represented by two non-dying placeholder agents.`  
*Gap:* 100-Founder population, energy drinking, autonomous governing, checkpoint rollback, resurrection, partner bonds and creation tools are not implemented.

**Worlds, realms, dimensions & creations — PARTIAL**  
Mortal creations inhabit worlds and cannot enter the House under ordinary rules. Realms connect worlds. Founder-made dimensions may require continuing energy to stay stable. Worlds have founder-managed day/night exposure.  
*Python:* `core.engine.WORLD_NAMES / PATCH_NAMES`  
*Gap:* Two test worlds are available; realm travel, dimensions, travel restrictions and construction of worlds are not implemented.

**Six Spirit actor classes — PARTIAL**  
Spirit actor classes are Knight, Emperor, Assassin, Castor, Archer, and Berserker; all can have agency and social relationships. They are distinct from spiritual validation in Soul Arts. Spirits age slowly and do not die of aging in the House or dimensions.  
*Python:* `core.engine.SPECIES["castor"]`  
*Gap:* Only Castor instructor is modeled; hybrid offspring, lifespan exceptions and other five Spirit roles are specified only.


### Physics

**Material identity, inventories & atomic transfers — OPERATIONAL**  
Material stocks and actor inventories are separate views of one conserved material ledger. Harvesting, hunting, trading and ownership movement transfer existing quantities rather than creating material. Invalid unfunded transactions must roll back.  
*Python:* `core.ledger.Book`  
*Gap:* Uses abstract single-component mass; not stoichiometric element/compound tracking or object-level inventory identity.

**Transformation-only universal energy toll — OPERATIONAL**  
The universal toll is triggered only by material state transitions, such as growth, digestion, chopping, crafting and death. A mere change of location or owner does not pay a transformation toll. Returned energy is routed to world, Underworld and House.  
*Python:* `core.ledger.Book.transform`  
*Gap:* Illustrative 10% toll and 20/10/70 allocation are test parameters, NOT immutable canon; reactions are coarse-grained.

**Sunlight, growth and energy accessibility — PARTIAL**  
Sunlight is emitted from a funded House reserve and powers accessible energy processes. Biological growth also requires matter. Usable reserves, chemical energy, embodied energy and dissipated heat are not freely interchangeable.  
*Python:* `core.engine.Simulation._daily_sun`  
*Gap:* Photosynthesis uses a coarse soil-to-plant proxy; chemistry, atmospheric carbon and nutrient cycling remain incomplete.


### Ecology

**All animals are individual Soul Network actors — PARTIAL**  
Every animal has identity, biological traits, energy budget, needs, memories, learned skills, relationships and Soul Network development. Species biology bounds possibilities; learning and experience determine competence.  
*Python:* `core.engine.Agent / SPECIES`  
*Gap:* No mating, gestation, multi-generational reproduction, inheritance or full species evolution yet.

**Species-specific learning, hunting and scavenging — PARTIAL**  
Pack predators can learn coordinated hunting; ambush predators can develop sneak and grapple techniques; foraging, shelter finding, migration and social knowledge are learned. Hunting success is not one fixed probability for all species.  
*Python:* `core.engine.Simulation._hunt / _eat_carrion`  
*Gap:* Simplified probabilities and limited skill spillovers; no juvenile learning or detailed territory systems.

**Nested overlapping spatial layers — PARTIAL**  
Physical world regions are indexed at multiple scales: element, material, space, habitat patches, region, world and realm. No layer may duplicate material or energy merely to provide a view.  
*Python:* `core.engine.PATCH_NAMES`  
*Gap:* Four habitat patches per world; no fine cells or element/material grid engine. Canvas positions are illustrative visual coordinates, not simulated geometry.


### Cognition

**Five needs and bounded choice — PARTIAL**  
Actors weigh physiological, safety, belonging, esteem, and self-development needs; motivations may conflict and there is no rigid Maslow ladder. Animals express analogous domains in species-dependent ways.  
*Python:* `core.engine.Simulation._choose_action`  
*Gap:* One-step utility, limited planning, limited social obligations, and abstract need units.

**Fear Logic, spiritual interpretation & commitment — PARTIAL**  
Fear may reflect danger, attraction, responsibility, conviction or desire, not merely anticipated loss. Spiritual orientation concerns how needs, fears, values, willingness and capability shape commitments and action.  
*Python:* `core.engine.Simulation._individual_conviction`  
*Gap:* Coarse fear/conviction numeric update; no reflective questioning process or complex persistent multi-step plans.


### Soul Network

**Experience, memory, schemas & trained Nodes — PARTIAL**  
Actions and observations create remembered experiences; meaningful repeated practice shapes skills and executive schemas and may develop Soul Network Nodes. Hearing or believing a lesson does not automatically confer trained mastery.  
*Python:* `core.engine.Simulation._experience`  
*Gap:* Basic practice-to-node threshold only; full schema/network development and validation remain specified.

**States, Kingdoms, Nations and Realms — SPECIFIED**  
A Kingdom coordinates at least three compatible States; a Nation organizes at least four States; a Realm requires at least four Kingdoms and an Emperor Node. Intrinsic Zodiac/Temple modes and Ascendant/Descendant Nodes affect development. Certain abilities require spiritual validation through commitments or vows. Exceptional overload requires disproportionate reality manipulation without adequate supplied energy/control.  
*Python:* `Canon register only`  
*Gap:* No State, Kingdom, Nation, Realm, zodiac, validation contract or exceptional overload engine.


### Society

**Directed families, friends, neighbors and mentors — PARTIAL**  
Actors have overlapping, directional social connections that shape information access and confidence; family, friends, relatives, caregivers, neighbors, adult figures, leaders and outsiders can each transmit different knowledge.  
*Python:* `core.engine.Simulation._build_social / _social_contact`  
*Gap:* Static directed edges and limited relationship categories; households, parenthood and trust updates are coarse or absent.

**Knowledge provenance, learning and tradition — PARTIAL**  
Exposure, acceptance, practice, capability, conformity and conviction are distinct. Information may be right or wrong; communication creates source-tagged memories. Traditions can persist through social learning across generations.  
*Python:* `core.engine.Simulation._social_contact`  
*Gap:* No generational succession or dynamic complex claim construction; only two test claims.


### RSW/CIV-OS

**Spirituality, religion and shared commitments — PARTIAL**  
Religion can emerge when personal spiritual interpretations of needs/fear are communicated and become shared durable meaning, values, ritual and obligation. RSW (meaning, legitimacy, limits) and PSW (instrumental capacity) are aspects of institutions, not separate departments.  
*Python:* `core.engine.Simulation._consider_shared_norm / _social_contact`  
*Gap:* Two predefined claims, very simple ritual events; belief formation lacks symbolic creativity and full dissent/coercion.

**Institutions with real members, rules and resources — PARTIAL**  
Organizations form through pledges, roles, resources, repeated activities, rules and continuity. They can adapt, reform, capture or dissolve. Track sacred object, declared vs operational model, legitimacy, cost incidence, governance and empirical outcomes.  
*Python:* `core.engine.Institution / _pledge / _institution_monitor`  
*Gap:* Food-sharing pools and one revision mechanism are working; no true independent governance, leadership succession, production projects or capture mechanics.


### Research

**Controlled model tests and adversarial audit — OPERATIONAL**  
Run seeded controls and ablations; require event provenance, mass/energy audits, distributions of costs, mechanism identification, registered failure criteria and hold-out testing. Simulations cannot be treated as empirical confirmation of real historical claims.  
*Python:* `tests/ and exported reports`  
*Gap:* Full registered 16x30x720-day experiment has not been run, and parameter fitting/calibration are preliminary.

## 11. Roadmap — what must happen before claiming full canon execution

1. Replace abstract one-component mass with material classes/elements and auditable biological chemistry; model reliable nutrient and water cycles.
2. Introduce pregnancy or species-appropriate reproduction, births, juvenile development, aging and inheritance; test predator–prey viability across generations.
3. Add Founder autonomy, roles, initial 100 actors, support pairs, energy consumption, creation powers and checkpoint resurrection.
4. Add persistent multistep goals, deeper personal interpretation, dynamic reciprocal relationships and family development.
5. Implement actual complex Soul Network executive schemas, States, validation contracts, hierarchy and exceptional overload safeguards.
6. Implement institutions with independently managed agricultural/water projects, leadership, voting, conflict, capture, and dissolution.
7. Activate nested material and spatial grids with authoritative stock references and coordinated update cadence.
8. Only then freeze rules, test cross-system integrity, and conduct confirmatory 480-run matched-seed factorial trials.
